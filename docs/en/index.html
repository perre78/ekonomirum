Furnished Apartments in Karlstad and Hammarö

We offer flexible and affordable furnished accommodation in Karlstad and Hammarö. Our housing solutions are suitable for companies, workers and private individuals looking for comfortable, fully furnished and move-in ready accommodation.

Accommodation for all needs

Many of our guests are construction workers and contractors working temporarily in the area, often through Swedish or international companies. At the same time, we also rent to private individuals and others who need a practical and ready-to-live-in home.

We offer both short-term and long-term rentals, depending on your needs.
